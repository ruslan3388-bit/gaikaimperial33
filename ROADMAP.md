# Roadmap

## v0.3 — Make the factory loop strategic
- Time-limited orders with different rewards.
- Market choices: sell raw stock now or process it for a higher-value product.
- Clear idle-state reasons when energy or inputs stop a workshop.
- Balance first upgrades and the first successful delivery with human playtests.

## v0.4 — Industrial systems
- Storage and transport capacity.
- Energy generation, fuel, and maintenance trade-offs.
- Research branches for metallurgy, automation, and logistics.

## v0.5 — Campaign
- Region map with resource sites, factories, and markets.
- Milestone contracts that unlock production sectors.
- Economic events that create temporary shortages or demand surges.

## Later — Telegram product features
- Server-side saves and account continuity.
- Daily challenges and leaderboards.
- Cooperative orders only after the solo economy is balanced.

## Design guardrails
- Keep the main loop understandable before adding systems.
- Avoid pay-to-win advantages.
- Test with real players before locking prices, timers, or progression.
